One relationship.
A broader perspective.

Our role extends beyond managing investments. We help clients bring clarity to complex financial circumstances, coordinate the right partners and make informed decisions with confidence.

Whether your priority is long-term structure, liquidity, the diversification of concentrated holdings or preparation for the next generation, we build the strategy around what matters to you.

Personal

Effective wealth management begins with understanding the person behind the portfolio. Our advice reflects your objectives, responsibilities, preferences and long-term ambitions.

International

With roots in Switzerland and an international outlook, we understand that wealth frequently crosses borders. We coordinate solutions with an awareness of different markets, institutions, cultures and regulatory environments.

Independent in perspective

We assess opportunities on their suitability for the client rather than limiting our thinking to the offering of a single bank or product provider. This open architecture allows us to evaluate solutions across institutions and asset classes.

Disciplined

Our investment decisions follow a structured process. Portfolio construction, implementation and monitoring are guided by clearly defined objectives and a consistent approach to risk.

Connected

Our network encompasses established custodian banks and selected specialists in investment management, wealth structuring, insurance, tax and legal matters. This enables us to bring together the expertise required for complex situations.

Transparent

Clients should always understand how their wealth is positioned, what risks they are taking and what they are paying for. We therefore place particular importance on clear reporting, open communication and ongoing oversight.

Scope of our role

Assets are held in the client's own name at a Swiss depositary bank of the client's choosing. Our power of attorney is limited to management and does not permit withdrawal or transfer to ourselves. The bank reports to the client independently of us.

What we do not do

We do not hold client assets. We do not issue or manufacture financial products. We do not lend, and we do not accept deposits. We do not accept retrocessions from third parties without disclosure and the client's agreement.
